David A. Robertson is an Indigenous author, podcaster, public speaker, and social advocate. He is a member of Norway House Cree Nation and lives and works in Winnipeg, Manitoba. His writing centers on Indigenous identity and culture, and on truth, healing, and reconciliation. This site looks at his path as a writer, his major contributions to Indigenous literature, and the impact his work has had on readers and classrooms across Canada.
