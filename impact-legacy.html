Impact & Legacy

Educational and Social Impact

Several of Robertson's books are used in classrooms across Canada. This brings Indigenous voices into school curricula and gives students opportunities to learn about Indigenous experiences from Indigenous perspectives. The use of 7 Generations: A Plains Cree Saga in schools and libraries shows that his writing has an educational impact beyond entertainment. It encourages meaningful discussion about Indigenous history, colonialism, and identity.

1
For Indigenous studentsEncountering characters, cultures, and experiences that reflect their own lives can strengthen representation, connection, and identity.
2
For non-Indigenous studentsHis books can develop a greater understanding of Indigenous histories, cultures, and contemporary experiences.
3
For classrooms and librariesHis work offers an accessible starting point for conversations about difficult histories and their continuing effects.

Cultural Impact

Robertson has contributed to contemporary Indigenous literature by bringing Indigenous characters and perspectives to younger audiences through books such as 7 Generations: A Plains Cree Saga, When We Were Alone, and The Misewa Saga. His writing provides some of the representation that he felt was missing from his own education and helps a new generation connect with Indigenous histories and cultures.

His work shows that Indigenous literature can address colonialism, racism, mental health, and other difficult subjects while also representing family, identity, culture, relationships, and everyday Indigenous experiences. This balance presents contemporary Indigenous cultures through their strength, complexity, and diversity rather than through hardship alone.

Impact Beyond His Own Books

As Editorial Director of Swift Water Books, Robertson helps create opportunities for Indigenous writers and illustrators, including new and emerging creators, to publish their work and reach young readers. By publishing picture books, graphic novels, chapter books, and young adult literature, the imprint makes Indigenous storytelling accessible across many age groups.

This work expands the impact of Robertson's career beyond his own voice. It supports a broader literary community in which Indigenous creators can present identity, history, culture, and lived experience through their own perspectives.

Overall Contribution

Robertson's contributions extend beyond the books he has written himself. Through his work as an author and as Editorial Director of Swift Water Books, he has helped expand the presence of Indigenous voices in contemporary children's and young adult literature. His books support learning and representation, while his publishing work opens doors for other Indigenous creators to share their own stories, perspectives, and experiences with a new generation of readers.
