Publishing & Advocacy

Swift Water Books

Robertson is the Editorial Director of Swift Water Books, an Indigenous led children's publishing imprint within Penguin Random House Canada. Swift Water Books publishes Indigenous writers and illustrators, including new and emerging creators. The imprint publishes many formats, including picture books, graphic novels, chapter books, and young adult literature, making Indigenous stories accessible to readers of different ages.

By helping other Indigenous creators reach young readers, Robertson expands representation beyond his own books and further advances understanding and reconciliation.
