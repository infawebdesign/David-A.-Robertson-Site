About the Author

Who He Is

David A. Robertson is a member of Norway House Cree Nation. He writes mainly for children and youth, and his books bring Indigenous identity, culture, and history to younger readers.

What Led Him to Writing

As a Cree child going to school in Winnipeg, Robertson felt disconnected from his heritage because his school didn't teach Indigenous cultures or histories. He also found textbooks boring, but was drawn to comics. He recognized that other children might also feel disconnected from their histories or find traditional textbooks unengaging. Because of this, he started writing graphic novels, a format that could both entertain young readers and teach them about Indigenous history and experiences.
